> Codes 520 to 527 are produced by Cloudflare when it cannot get a valid response from your origin server.

521 means your server refused the connection, 522 means the connection timed out, 523 means Cloudflare could not route to your IP, 524 means your server was too slow to finish responding, and 525 or 526 mean the TLS handshake with your origin failed. The fastest way to diagnose any of them is to bypass Cloudflare and request your origin IP directly. If the origin answers, the problem is between Cloudflare and your server.
