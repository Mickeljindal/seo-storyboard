Egress is the fee for data leaving your storage, every image loaded, video streamed, or backup restored.

It's usually free to upload (ingress) and metered to serve out (egress). Because egress scales with traffic, it often becomes the biggest line on a storage bill. Some object storage doesn't meter egress at all, Kloudbean's built-in S3-compatible storage among them, so the bill reflects what you store, not how popular you get.
