> Use 301 when the move is genuinely permanent and you are certain of the destination: a retired URL, a domain change, a canonical host.

Use 302 for anything temporary or anything you might reverse: maintenance pages, A/B tests, geographic or device routing, a promotion. The reason to be careful with 301 is caching. Browsers may remember a permanent redirect indefinitely, so a wrong one keeps sending returning visitors to the wrong place even after you fix the server.
