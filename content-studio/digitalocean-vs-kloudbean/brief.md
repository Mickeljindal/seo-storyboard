# Brief — DigitalOcean vs Kloudbean

Cluster 1. Primary kw: DigitalOcean vs Kloudbean. Secondary: DigitalOcean alternative, managed DigitalOcean hosting, Droplet vs managed hosting, DigitalOcean App Platform alternative.
Intent: comparison/commercial. A developer who likes DigitalOcean's pricing but is weighing raw Droplets (full control, full ops) vs App Platform (PaaS limits/pricing) vs a managed layer.
Angle: DO is a genuine favorite — fair pricing, great docs. Two DO experiences: Droplet (you own the ops) and App Platform (managed but PaaS). Kloudbean is a managed layer that can even run ON DigitalOcean (DO is one of its providers), giving you DO's infra with one-click deploy + managed DB/SSL/backups. Honest: if you want a raw droplet and enjoy ops, DO direct is great; Kloudbean is for people who want managed without becoming an ops person. Not a knock on DO — it's a different job.
Distinct from other comparisons: this one leans on "you don't have to choose — Kloudbean can sit on top of DO." Uses hero + console git-deployment screenshot (deploy-experience section is the crux).
Slug: digitalocean-vs-kloudbean. Images: hero.png + ../assets/console/git-deployment.png. Links: pillar, Lovable guide, pricing.
Honesty guardrails: Linux stacks only (not Windows/.NET/IIS); you own the app, Kloudbean manages server/stack/SSL/backups (server-level backups default; daily + DR on Premium/Enterprise). No blurbs.
