> Yes, Postgres does real full-text search.

It turns text into normalized lexemes (`tsvector`), parses the query (`tsquery`), matches with the `@@` operator, ranks with `ts_rank`, and stays fast behind a `GIN` index. For most apps that's plenty, and it's one less system than Elasticsearch. Graduate to a dedicated search engine when search becomes a core product surface, not before.
