# Brief — GDPR Compliant Hosting (myth-vs-reality)

Cluster 10. Primary kw: gdpr compliant hosting / gdpr hosting / is my hosting gdpr compliant. Intent: informational (EU-serving businesses). Pillar-driven.
FORMAT: Myth-vs-reality (bust 6 GDPR hosting myths). Opener = GDPR sounds scary; most of the fear is myths. It's process, not panic.
Myths→Reality: 1 "GDPR only applies to EU companies" → applies to anyone processing EU residents' data 2 "data must be stored in the EU" → EU region is simplest but transfers allowed with safeguards (adequacy/SCCs) 3 "if my host is GDPR compliant, I'm compliant" → shared: host=processor via DPA covers infra; you=controller own consent/lawful basis/retention 4 "GDPR is just hosting location" → mostly your practices (consent, data minimization, user rights access/erasure, breach notification) 5 "it's a one-time setup" → ongoing 6 "small sites are exempt" → no blanket exemption. Then what hosting actually contributes + honest boundary + NOT legal advice.
SCREENSHOT: env-vars.png (control access to the credentials/config that reach personal data).
Honesty woven (CENTRAL): compliance shared (controller vs processor); host provides technical controls (EU-region residency, SSL, access controls, backups, audit trail on enterprise) + a DPA any serious host puts in place; you own consent/lawful basis/rights/retention; NOT legal advice; don't claim a "GDPR certified" host.
