## Information gain
The reframe: agencies already do maintenance, they just do it unbilled and reactively. A retainer
turns invisible, reactive work into visible, recurring, proactive revenue.
1. The deliverables list: what a real WP maintenance retainer includes (updates on staging first,
   backups + tested restore, security monitoring, uptime, small content changes, a monthly report).
2. Updates are the core and the risk: plugin/theme/core updates are why sites break, so they must run
   on staging first, not click-update-on-prod-and-pray. This is the operational heart.
3. Tiering that maps to real work, not arbitrary bronze/silver/gold: what genuinely changes across
   tiers (update cadence, support response, hours of changes, reporting depth).
4. Scope creep is the killer: define "included" vs "billable" explicitly (a small text change vs a new
   feature), or the retainer becomes unlimited-work-for-a-fixed-fee.
5. The monthly report is what actually retains the client: it makes invisible work visible, which is
   why care plans churn less. "Here's what we did" is the renewal.
6. The SLA/response commitment: what "we'll handle it" actually promises.

Angle: reframe-invisible-work-as-revenue, the-monthly-report-is-the-product (proof-of-value), name-the-
tradeoff (unlimited scope vs defined scope).
