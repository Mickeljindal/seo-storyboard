## Original value competitors do not have
- **Rejects the standard framing in the first paragraph.** "WordPress is more popular, Joomla is more
  powerful" is not a decision framework. Replaces it with two specific questions that actually settle it.
- **Names Joomla's two real advantages concretely and fairly**: granular ACL in core (user groups,
  access levels, per-action permissions, no plugin) and native multilingual (language associations,
  per-language menus). Explains WHY each matters, e.g. with WordPress the security model of the site
  ends up depending on a role-management plugin staying maintained.
- **Ecosystem framed as concrete project risk rather than a soft factor**: the specific integration you
  need, and replacing your developer in eighteen months. That converts a vague "bigger community" claim
  into something a buyer can act on.
- **The security question answered against the grain.** WordPress is not inherently insecure; core is
  patched quickly and plugins are the problem. Joomla's smaller attack surface is a consequence of a
  smaller ecosystem, not better engineering. Conclusion: neither is meaningfully safer when maintained,
  but the SHAPE of the maintenance differs (auditing plugins vs maintaining more custom code with fewer
  people who understand it).
- **An operational-cost section competitors skip entirely**: both are self-hosted PHP apps, so the real
  work is identical, and the platforms diverge on update frequency and blast radius.
- **A stop-at-the-first-match decision path** with eight branches, including the override ("check which
  platform has a maintained integration for your specific requirement, that beats everything above")
  and an explicit honest default ("still undecided: WordPress, and it is not close").
- **Migration treated as a rebuild, not a conversion**, with the specific warning that anyone offering
  one-click migration between these two means content export, which is the easy part. Plus the redirect
  mapping as the step that protects rankings and is most often skipped.
- **A stated honest asymmetry in our own product**: Kloudbean staging covers WordPress and Laravel, so
  picking WordPress gets you one-click staging for precisely the update-breaks-the-site risk the article
  just described, and on Joomla you would handle it yourself. Volunteering that is more persuasive than
  hiding it, and it is required by the facts file.
