Do I Need Separate Frontend and Backend Servers?

Usually no. Most apps run fine with the front end and back end on one server, or even as a single full-stack app, and splitting them onto separate servers adds CORS, a second deploy, and cross-origin auth to manage. Split when a part genuinely needs its own home: a static front end on a CDN, a front end and back end that scale or ship on different schedules, or clearly different stacks.
