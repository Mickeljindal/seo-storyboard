What Is High Availability? How Apps Stay Up When Things Fail

High availability (HA) means a system stays available even when a component fails, because there's no single point of failure and traffic fails over to a healthy replica. You get there with redundancy (more than one of everything that matters), a load balancer running health checks, and automatic failover. HA is not backups and not disaster recovery.
