Managed vs Unmanaged Hosting: Who Actually Runs the Server?

Unmanaged hosting rents you a raw server and hands you every operations task on it: patching, SSL, firewall, backups, monitoring, and the 2am pager. Managed hosting runs all of that for you on the same class of machine, so you touch your app, not the OS.
