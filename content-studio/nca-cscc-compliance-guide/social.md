> CSCC-1:2019 is the National Cybersecurity Authority's set of minimum cybersecurity requirements for critical systems in Saudi Arabia.

It extends the Essential Cybersecurity Controls (ECC-1:2018), and ECC compliance is a prerequisite, so CSCC is additional to ECC rather than a replacement. It contains 4 main domains, 21 subdomains, 32 main controls, and 73 subcontrols. It applies to organisations that own or operate systems they have identified as critical.
