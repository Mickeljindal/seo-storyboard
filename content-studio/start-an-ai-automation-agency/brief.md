# Brief: start-an-ai-automation-agency
## Angle (opportunity / business playbook)
For the freelancer or aspiring agency owner who wants to start an AI automation agency: building
workflow automations and LLM integrations for clients with n8n, Make, Zapier. Covers the offer, who
buys, the tooling stack, delivery, and how to host client automations without becoming a full-time
sysadmin. Teach-first; product appears once at the "host client workflows" step plus the CTA.

## Intent
Informational / aspirational business playbook. Reader wants a realistic path to a first client and
a delivery model that holds up. Payoff is the offer + tooling + delivery + hosting model.

## Information gain (one sentence)
It treats automation as a service business (the moat is delivery, not the tools), gives an honest
hosted-vs-self-hosted tool tradeoff instead of an always-win, and names client isolation as the ops
mistake that bites hardest, which hype-driven "start an agency" content skips.

## Founder opinion + anti-pattern (honesty)
- Opinion: automation is a service business, not a software one; pick one narrow offer and one tool
  you know before spreading across all three.
- Anti-pattern: running every client on one shared account with no separation of access, data, or
  billing. Real ops/security mistake, no invented customer.
- Fair to tools: n8n Cloud, Make, and Zapier are legitimate ways to start; self-host n8n when volume,
  data control, or margins justify it. One measured line per tool.
- No income promises, per-client revenue figures, or market-size numbers.
