> Expose two endpoints.

A *liveness* check (like `/healthz`) that's cheap and just confirms the process is responding, used to decide whether to restart it. And a *readiness* check (like `/readyz`) that verifies critical dependencies such as the database and Redis, used to decide whether to send traffic. Keep dependency checks out of liveness, or a database blip will restart healthy apps and turn a small problem into an outage.
