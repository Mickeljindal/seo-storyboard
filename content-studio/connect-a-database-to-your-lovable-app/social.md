How to Connect a Database to Your Lovable App the Right Way

Your Lovable app already talks to Supabase Postgres by default, and for many apps that is a real database. To connect your own, you have two honest paths: keep Supabase but run it on infrastructure you own, or add a managed PostgreSQL or MySQL and reach it through a small server. Either way the connection lives in a server-side `DATABASE_URL`, never in front-end code.
