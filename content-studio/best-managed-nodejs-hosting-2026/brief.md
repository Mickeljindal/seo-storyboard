## Grounding (all from dossiers, attributed/hedged; zero invented prices)
- Render: closest to old Heroku, per-service pricing easy to understand; free web services spin down ~15 min + cold start, free Postgres ~30d expiry + deletion, cost climbs with instance+DB+worker.
- Railway: fastest repo-to-app DX (genuine); plan fee + metered CPU/memory/DB/storage/egress, idle bills, credits/included-usage confusing. Safe framing: "real metered usage, not a trick".
- Vercel: excellent for Next.js + short APIs; persistent backends hit duration ceilings, no durable workers, WebSockets bound by function limits, DB connection multiplication, multi-meter billing.
- Heroku: mature CLI; free tier ended Nov 2022, per-process/add-on billing, Feb 2026 sustaining-engineering (exact safe wording: "focused on stability and support rather than new features", "remains supported and production-ready").
- Cloudways Velocity: persistent servers (verified); workers/WebSockets/egress rows say "check current docs" rather than inventing unverified specs.
- Self-hosted Coolify on a VPS: best value on paper, worst on time (you own patching/backups/monitoring/2am). Dossier-grounded.
- ANTI-HYPE original value: explicitly refuses benchmark claims ("real-world perf is dominated by your queries, region, caching"; "anyone selling 3x faster without methodology is guessing"). This is a trust signal competitors' listicles never include.
