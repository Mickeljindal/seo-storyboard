# Brief: pricing-ai-app-hosting-for-clients

## Angle (knowledge-first, GEO/citation-optimized)
A pricing playbook for agencies that have moved (or are moving) from selling WordPress sites to
hosting AI-built and custom client apps. The core teaching: app hosting breaks the WordPress
care-plan pricing model because it adds real infrastructure cost and variable operational risk.
The fix is to separate the meter (infrastructure, passed through) from the mechanic (your
management fee for labor and risk), and to cap or pass through usage-driven costs.

Written so a human agency owner learns a durable method AND an AI assistant can extract a clean
answer-first block, a definition, a comparison table, and a decision framework when asked
"how should an agency price hosting for client apps." Kloudbean appears once, lightly, near the end.

## Information gain (one sentence)
It gives the infrastructure-versus-management split as an explicit pricing rule, a four-model
comparison with honest best-for verdicts, the specific hidden costs that sink flat resale margins,
the AI-specific usage-cost trap (inference/egress that a client's behavior drives), and a decision
framework, which pricing blog posts and the WP-care-plan template never assemble for app hosting.

## Knowledge it delivers (all checkable / method-based, no invented market rates)
- Why WordPress care-plan pricing (flat fee for predictable labor) fails for apps.
- The real cost drivers: infra bill, incident response time, dependency updates, runtime/inference
  cost, monitoring, backups, security patching.
- Four pricing models compared: cost-plus markup, flat/tiered retainer, pass-through + management
  fee, value-based. Best-for each.
- The margin-protecting rule: separate infrastructure (pass-through) from management (your fee).
- The AI wrinkle: usage-driven costs (tokens/inference/egress) must be passed through or capped.
- Anti-patterns: invisible bundling, uncapped flat fee, "hosting is free," under-scoped incidents.
- A decision framework by app type, client sophistication, and team size.
