> A connection pool keeps a small set of database connections open and reuses them, instead of opening a fresh one for every request.

You need it the moment your app runs more than a couple of concurrent workers, or the second you see `too many connections`. Fix it at three levels: a driver pool in your app (pg `Pool`, HikariCP, SQLAlchemy), a dedicated pooler like PgBouncer when many app instances hit one database, and a pooler in front of serverless functions to tame connection storms.
