# Brief: how-to-build-a-saas-without-a-technical-cofounder
## Angle (myth-vs-reality / honest decision guide)
For the non-technical or solo founder who wants to build and run a SaaS without hiring a technical
cofounder or a DevOps engineer. Thesis: AI coding tools help you build, and managed hosting runs the
infrastructure, so the missing "technical cofounder" is really two solvable problems (build the app,
operate the servers). Honest about what still can't be outsourced. Product appears once as the
managed-infra layer plus CTA.

## Intent
Informational / decision. Reader wants an honest read on whether they can build+run a SaaS solo now,

## Information gain (one sentence)
It reframes "technical cofounder" as two separable problems (building vs operating) and draws the
honest shared-responsibility line (managed covers server/stack/SSL/backups/patching; you still own
app code, data, product, support, and app-level security), which motivational "you can do it" posts skip.

## Founder opinion + anti-pattern (honesty)
- Opinion: you don't need a technical cofounder to start; you need to not run your own raw
  infrastructure. But a cofounder still helps for deep custom engineering and hard scaling (not always-win).
- Anti-pattern: assuming "managed" means you can ignore your own app's security and your data logic.
- No success-rate stats, founder counts, or income promises.
